# Campaign readiness handoff

Classification: simplified checklist derived from the inspected separation between construction, QA and activation.

1. Confirm the tenant, approved offer and intended audience.
2. Confirm that required copy pools and variables are populated.
3. Verify sender readiness and the applicable capacity policy.
4. Check that copy variants stay inside the same offer/campaign.
5. Inspect the actual provider sequence, schedule and suppression settings.
6. Record launch approval separately from campaign creation.
7. After activation, verify native sending and reply receipts within the agreed scope.

Creating a campaign does not prove that it sent. A reply does not prove a meeting was held. This checklist contains no universal sender-volume or warmup recommendation.
