# Copy revision with an approved claim

Classification: synthetic worked example. The company, offer and outcome below are invented.

**Approved brief:** a reporting service may offer software finance teams a plan targeting a 20% reduction in manual reporting time. No guarantee is approved.

**Rejected candidate:** “We guarantee your team will cut reporting time by 50%. Want to see the plan?”

Why it fails: the percentage changed and a guarantee was added. Fluent wording does not authorize either claim.

**Revised candidate:** “We can build your finance team a reporting plan targeting 20% less manual reporting time. Would you like to see how the plan works?”

The reviewer still needs to assess audience fit and clarity. This example illustrates the process; the exact executable fixture and evaluator response live in the Script Factory examples folder. Neither version is evidence of an actual customer result.
